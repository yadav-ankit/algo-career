
Monotonic stack is one of those patterns where after 10–15 problems, you start spotting it instantly.

What is a monotonic stack?

A stack that maintains elements in sorted order:

Increasing stack

Top always has larger values:

1
3
5
8   <- top

When smaller comes:

new = 4

pop 8
pop 5

push 4

stack:
1 3 4

Used for finding smaller elements.

Decreasing stack

Stack:

8
6
4
2

When bigger comes:

new = 7

pop 2
pop 4
pop 6

push 7

stack:
8 7

Used for finding greater elements.
